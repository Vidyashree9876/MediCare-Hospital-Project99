ABOUT US

About MediCare Hospital

MediCare is a hospital enquiry and appointment-information project designed to help patients find basic information and contact the hospital team more easily.

💙

Patient-first approach

Clear information, simple navigation and a straightforward way to send questions to hospital staff.

🩺

Listed departments

General Medicine, Gynecology and Orthopedics are the three departments currently listed in this project.

📆

Appointment requests

Patients can submit appointment requests through the online form. Availability should be confirmed by the hospital.

💬

Patient enquiries

Use the Enquiry page to send a question with your contact details. Staff can review it in the Admin panel and record a response.

🔐

Respectful handling

Share only the information needed to answer your enquiry. Do not submit passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive medical details.

ℹ️

Project information

Doctor schedules, services and contact details shown here are project-listed information and should be verified by hospital management before real-world use.

This website is a demonstration project. Contact details marked as demo placeholders must be replaced with verified hospital contact information before public use.
